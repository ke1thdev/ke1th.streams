A quick note for iPhone & iPad users

Apple devices have strict built-in restrictions that prevent them from playing some premium channels.

To make things easy, we've added a small icon next to channels that will not work on iOS.

But don't worry! All of these premium channels still work perfectly fine on Android phones, Windows PCs, and Mac computers!
