Before you start watching

ke1th.streams itself is completely ad-free! However, the video players might redirect you to an ad page or open a pop-up when you click the Play button.

The good news is that you can easily block these using an ad blocker for a clean experience!

  • iOS: I prefer using a Safari extension like AdGuard (enable in Settings), but you can also use the Brave browser.
  • Android: I highly recommend using the Brave browser to easily bypass the ads.
  • Desktop/Laptop: You can install an adblocker extension like AdGuard on your browser.

Quick tip: If a stubborn ad still opens in a new tab when you press Play, just close it and click Play again!
