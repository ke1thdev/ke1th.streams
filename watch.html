Before you start watching

ke1th.streams itself is completely ad-free! However, the video player might redirect you to an ad page or open a pop-up when you click the Play button.

The good news is the Default Server works perfectly with ad blockers for a clean experience.

  • iOS: I prefer using a Safari extension like AdGuard (enable in Settings), but you can also use the Brave browser.
  • Android: I recommend using the Brave browser to easily bypass ads.
  • Desktop/Laptop: You can install an adblocker extension (like uBlock Origin) on your browser.

Quick tip: Without an ad blocker, just close any ad tab that opens when you press Play, and click Play again. (Note: Server 2 may still show ads).
